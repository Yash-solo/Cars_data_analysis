# Cars_data_analysis
I conducted a focused data analysis to understand which factors influence car selling prices over a 15-year period (2003–2018).

The analysis reveals that selling price is mainly impacted by fuel type, vehicle age (depreciation), seller type, and transmission.

Diesel cars have the highest average selling price, indicating stronger resale value compared to petrol and CNG vehicles. A key factor affecting price is depreciation, as the present price of cars consistently declines over time, directly lowering selling price.

Seller type also plays a role, with dealer-listed cars generally achieving better pricing outcomes than individual sellers, likely due to better market reach and trust. Additionally, manual transmission cars dominate the market, which influences overall pricing trends due to higher supply.

In conclusion, selling price is not driven by a single factor but by a combination of fuel preference, time-based depreciation, seller credibility, and market demand patterns.
